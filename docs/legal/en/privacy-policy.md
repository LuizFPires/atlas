# Atlas Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 · November 19, 2026**

> English translation for customers of the English site (use-atlas-ai.com). It is under legal review. If there is any difference between this text and the Portuguese original, the Portuguese original prevails.

This Policy explains how **PL Participações LTDA**, Brazilian company registration (CNPJ) 30.484.256/0001-82, located at Avenida Guaratã, 1885, Sala 12, Calafate, Belo Horizonte/MG, Brazil, ZIP 30411-516, processes the personal data of people who visit the Atlas websites (atlas-ia.app.br and use-atlas-ai.com) and of people who purchase and use Atlas. **Controller:** PL Participações LTDA.
**Data Protection Officer (DPO):** Luiz Felipe Souza Alvarenga Pires · luizfsap@gmail.com.

## 1. What data we process and why

| Data | When | What for | Legal basis (LGPD, art. 7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name or nickname, company name, description of the business, email | At sign-up, right after the purchase | Create the account, send access, personalize Atlas (call you by name, suggest ideas for your field) | Performance of a contract (V) |
| Billing data: name, tax ID (Brazilian CPF or CNPJ, when paying in reais), amounts, dates and payment status | At purchase and on monthly payments | Charge, issue invoices and meet tax obligations | Performance of a contract (V) and legal obligation (II) |
| Card or payment account data | At purchase | Process the payment. **It stays only with the payment processor** (Stripe); PL Participações LTDA does not store card data | Performance of a contract (V) |
| License data: computer identifier, machine name, operating system version, activation and transfer dates, last access | At activation and during use | Validate the license, allow changing computers and prevent copying and passing it on | Performance of a contract (V) and legitimate interest (IX), to prevent fraud |
| Project usage data: the stage each Project is at, percentage completed, pauses, technical errors and the step where the Customer stopped | During use | Show progress, warn when something gets stuck and improve Atlas | Legitimate interest (IX) |
| News preferences on the phone | At setup | Send Project news at the chosen frequency | Performance of a contract (V) |
| Website browsing data: IP address, pages visited, cookies | When visiting the Atlas websites | Operation and security of the website and, if you accept, statistics | Legitimate interest (IX) and consent (I) for non-essential cookies |

## 2. What PL Participações LTDA does **not** receive

- **The code and data of your Projects.** They stay on your computer and in your accounts (GitHub, Supabase, Vercel and others). PL Participações LTDA has no access.
- **The content of your conversations with Atlas, microphone recordings and screenshots sent to Atlas Help.** They are processed on your computer and by Anthropic's Claude, **in your own Claude account**, following Anthropic's privacy policy. PL Participações LTDA does not store this content.
- **Your passwords** for Claude, GitHub, domain registrars, app stores and other services.

## 3. Who we share data with

We only share data with those who need it for Atlas to work:
- the payment processor (Stripe);
- the email delivery service (Resend);
- the hosting providers of the website and the license server (Vercel and Supabase);
- public authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

## 4. International transfer

Some providers may store data on servers outside Brazil. In these cases, we adopt the safeguards provided for in article 33 of the LGPD, such as data protection contractual clauses.

## 5. How long we keep data

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Sign-up and license | While the account exists. After that, up to 6 months for security and fraud prevention |
| Tax and billing data | For the period required by tax law (generally 5 years) |
| Project usage data | Up to 12 months |

After these periods, the data is deleted or anonymized.

## 6. Security

We use technical and administrative measures to protect data, such as encrypted connections, restricted access and access logs. No system is 100% secure. If there is an incident that may bring relevant risk, we will notify you and the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD), as required by law.

## 7. Your rights (LGPD, art. 18)

You may, at any time:
- confirm whether we process your data and access it;
- correct incomplete or outdated data;
- request anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data;
- request portability;
- know who we share it with;
- withdraw consent, when consent is the legal basis;
- object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To do so, write to **luizfsap@gmail.com**. We reply within 15 (fifteen) days. You can also file a complaint with the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or with the data protection authority of your country.

## 8. The systems you create

In the systems you build and publish with Atlas, **you are the controller of your users' data** (customers, employees and others). It is up to you to inform those users and comply with the data protection laws that apply to you. Atlas can generate a privacy policy template for your system, which you must check before publishing.

## 9. Children

Atlas is intended for people over 18 and for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

If this Policy changes in a relevant way, we will notify you by email and in the app before the change takes effect.
